A Craftsman's Heart

Built with Honesty, Carved with Pride

"A house is built once in a lifetime. Furniture should not just fill an empty corner; it should be strong enough for your grandchildren to sit upon."

My father started his journey 15 years ago with nothing more than a bag of hand chisels, a wooden hand plane, and a bicycle. In the early days, he traveled miles across dirt roads between small villages, fixing broken carts, repairing creaking temple doors, and making simple wooden beds for farming families.

Over the last decade and a half, modern tools and high-gloss laminates have arrived, but his fundamental values have never changed. He still touches every piece of timber with his bare hands to check moisture and grain direction. He refuses to use cheap, hollow boards that swell up during monsoon season.

Because he works directly with his hands and travels between villages, families trust him like a brother. When you hire him, you are supporting an honest local craftsman who will treat your home with the exact same care he gives his own.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you plan your woodwork project with confidence.

Do you charge for visiting nearby villages and taking measurements?
No! Measurement visits to surrounding villages and towns are completely free of charge. We bring wood catalogues and tape measures to your doorstep, discuss your requirements, and provide a clear quote without any obligation.
How do you ensure the wood won't bend or get termite attacks?
We exclusively use chemically seasoned timber and certified BWP (Boiling Water Proof) 710 Grade marine plywood. We also apply anti-termite and anti-fungal wood primer to all hidden joints and wall contact faces before final installation.
Can you make furniture from a photo I found on Pinterest or YouTube?
Yes, absolutely! Show us any photo or video on your phone. With 15 years of master carpentry experience, my father can replicate the exact joinery, proportions, and laminate color scheme customized for your specific room dimensions.
How long does a complete modular kitchen or wardrobe project take?
A standard modular kitchen or 2 wardrobes typically takes 10 to 14 working days. Most of the heavy cutting, laminating, and edge-banding is done in our workshop to minimize dust and disturbance in your home, with on-site assembly taking only 2-3 days.
What brands of hardware and hinges do you use?
We rely on trusted industry brands including Hettich, Hafele, Ebco, and Godrej. All hydraulic drawers and soft-close hinges come with the manufacturer's warranty against rusting and mechanical failure.
What are your payment terms?
We follow a simple, transparent milestone structure: 40% advance to procure raw timber and certified plywood, 40% upon framework completion and delivery to site, and the remaining 20% only after complete installation, final polishing, and your full satisfaction.
