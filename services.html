Homeowner's Guide

Understanding Wood & Plywood Quality

Most homeowners don't know the difference between cheap commercial board and marine plywood. Here is our honest transparency guide so you never get cheated:

Best for Kitchens & Bathrooms

BWP 710 Marine Grade Plywood

Boiling Water Proof plywood bonded with synthetic phenolic resins. Can survive 72 hours in boiling water without de-laminating. Completely immune to kitchen moisture, steam, and termites.

Best for Living & Bedrooms

BWR 303 Grade Plywood

Boiling Water Resistant plywood ideal for bedroom wardrobes, TV units, and beds. Highly durable against room humidity, dimensionally stable, and cost-effective.

Heirloom Doors & Cots

Seasoned Solid Teak (Sagwan)

Naturally rich in natural wood oils that repel termites and rot. Has beautiful golden-brown grain patterns that darken gracefully over decades. Handcrafted with traditional mortise joints.

Branded Hardware

Hettich / Hafele / Ebco

We never use cheap unbranded hinges that rust or sag after six months. We only fit tested German/Indian brand hydraulic soft-close hinges, heavy telescopic channels, and brass handles.
